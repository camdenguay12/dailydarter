Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how The Daily Darter (dailydarter.com) handles information when you visit and play the game.

Game progress on your device

The site uses browser local storage to remember your guesses, completed challenges, difficulty selection, and game statistics. This information is stored in your browser, not in a game account. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data. Clearing data may also remove your saved progress.

Advertising and cookies

The Daily Darter includes Google AdSense advertising code. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable law and your choices. Google may process information such as your IP address, browser or device details, and interactions with advertisements.

Learn more at How Google uses information for advertising and Google My Ad Center, where you can manage some advertising preferences.

Third-party services

The site is delivered through web hosting and uses third-party resources, including Google Fonts and a read-only player-data feed. These providers may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Your choices

You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, adjust available Google advertising preferences, and clear this site's local storage. Blocking certain storage or scripts may affect how the site remembers game progress or displays ads.

Children

The Daily Darter is a general-audience darts trivia game and is not designed specifically for children.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy if the site's features or services change. The revision date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Questions

For privacy questions, please use the project's GitHub issues page. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly.
