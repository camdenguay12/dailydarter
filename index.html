Welcome to The Daily Darter!

Find the mystery darts player in six guesses. Search for a player to reveal five clues: PDC rank, country, age, best PDC win tier, and throwing hand.

  • 🟩 Green: Exact match.
  • 🟨 Yellow: Close match — rank within 10, age within 2 years, country on the same continent, or win tier one level away.
  • 🟥 Red: Different — not close enough.
  • ⬆ / ⬇ Arrows: The mystery player has a higher/lower numeric value. For PDC rank, a smaller number means a better ranking.
  • Gray: A statistic is unavailable.

Easy: Answer is among the top 70. Hard: Answer can be any of the 135 players. All players remain guessable.
